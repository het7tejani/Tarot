If your birthday is April 22, your zodiac sign is Taurus. Taurus runs from about April 20 to May 20, so April 22 sits two days inside it. Aries, the sign before, ends around April 19 or 20, and that edge is the only one that matters near this date.

This guide covers what Taurus says about an April 22 birthday, how the date fits the sign and which tarot card matches.

## Why April 22 is Taurus

The Sun enters Taurus on April 19, 20 or 21, depending on the year. April 22 falls after all three, so the sign is the same every year. People born on April 19, 20 or 21 are the ones who should check the exact entry time for their birth year.

You will sometimes see April 22 described as an Aries-Taurus cusp birthday. The cusp label is popular, but astrology does not treat it as a separate sign. Your Sun sign is Taurus. For the full range and the edge days, read our [Taurus zodiac dates guide](/blog/taurus-zodiac-dates).

## What an April 22 birthday looks like in Taurus

Taurus is an earth sign ruled by Venus, the planet astrology links to pleasure, money and taste. A Taurus is described as steady, sensual and stubborn. April 22 Taurus people tend to like good food, soft fabrics and a plan that stays the same. They decide slowly and rarely change their mind once they have.

The strength of the sign is reliability. A Taurus keeps the promise, pays the bill and stays through the long project. The weak point is resistance to change. A Taurus can defend an old habit simply because it is theirs. For more on the sign's traits, see our guide to [Taurus personality traits](/blog/taurus-personality-traits).

## April 22 and the Taurus decan

Each sign splits into three parts of about ten days, called decans. In the modern decan system, the first one belongs to the sign itself. April 22 falls in the first Taurus decan, which runs from about April 20 to April 29 and is ruled by Venus.

The second decan, from about April 30 to May 9, borrows from Virgo and adds detail and practicality. The third, from about May 10 to May 20, borrows from Capricorn and adds ambition. An April 22 birthday gets the pure Venus version of Taurus: the most sensual and comfort-loving part of the sign.

## Tarot card for Taurus

[The Hierophant](/tarot-card-meanings/the-hierophant) is the card most often paired with Taurus. It stands for tradition, shared values and trusted routines, which suits a sign that values what lasts. The card does not demand obedience. It asks what customs you actually want to keep.

[The Empress](/tarot-card-meanings/the-empress) also fits, since Venus rules both. The card is about comfort, growth and taking care of the body and the home. If you want a card for your own day, [draw a free tarot card](/free-tarot). It takes under a minute and needs no account. Tarot works as a reflection tool and does not predict events.

## Compatibility for an April 22 birthday

Earth and water signs usually read as the easiest match for Taurus. Virgo and Capricorn share the pace, and Cancer and Pisces share the love of home. Scorpio sits directly opposite Taurus and the pair tends to be intense. Our post on [Taurus and Libra compatibility](/blog/taurus-and-libra-compatibility) covers a pairing under the same ruler, Venus.

Fire and air signs can work with patience on both sides. For a particular pair, use the [zodiac compatibility guide](/zodiac-compatibility).

## Using the date well

Late April is the middle of spring in the Northern Hemisphere, and Taurus is the sign most tied to it: planting, rebuilding and slowing down. A simple use of the date is to pick one routine you want to keep and one you have outgrown, then write both down. Taurus energy works best with a small, specific change rather than a full reset.
