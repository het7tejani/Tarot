If your birthday is August 24, your zodiac sign is Virgo. The sign has no doubt attached to it: Virgo runs from about August 23 to September 22, and August 24 sits one day inside that range. The Sun is in Virgo on this date every year, including leap years.

This guide covers what the sign says about an August 24 birthday, how the date fits into Virgo season and which tarot card matches the sign.

## Why August 24 is always Virgo

The Sun leaves Leo and enters Virgo on August 22 or 23, depending on the year. That means August 22 and August 23 are the dates that can shift between Leo and Virgo. August 24 falls after both, so it never changes sign.

If you were born on August 23, your sign depends on the exact entry time in your birth year, and a [birth chart](/birth-chart) settles it. For August 24, you can stop checking. The full range is laid out in our [Virgo zodiac dates guide](/blog/virgo-zodiac-dates).

## What an August 24 birthday looks like in Virgo

Virgo is an earth sign ruled by Mercury, the planet astrology links to speech, detail and sorting information. People born in the first days of Virgo are often described as the most direct of the sign, since they sit closest to Leo's confidence and carry a little of it into Virgo's precision.

In practice, that description points to a few habits. An August 24 Virgo tends to notice what is wrong with a plan before anyone else does, and to say so politely. They keep lists. They check the spelling on the card before sending it. When they help, they help in specific ways: they fix the spreadsheet, drive you to the airport, remember the allergy.

The usual weak point is self-criticism. A Virgo can spend a week on a mistake nobody else noticed. Astrology treats this as the cost of the sign's attention, which cannot be switched off for their own work.

## August 24 and the Virgo decan

Western astrology splits each sign into three parts of about ten days, called decans. In the modern decan system, the first part belongs to the sign itself. August 24 sits in the first decan of Virgo, which runs from about August 23 to September 1. This decan is ruled by Mercury itself, the sign's own planet, so the Virgo traits run at full strength here.

The other two decans bring in Saturn and Venus. Saturn adds discipline in the middle of the sign, and Venus adds warmth and taste at the end. An August 24 birthday gets neither, which is why the sign's reputation for tidy thinking fits this date well.

## Tarot card for Virgo

The card most often paired with Virgo is [The Hermit](/tarot-card-meanings/the-hermit), a figure who walks alone with a lantern and studies one thing carefully. The Hermit's lesson matches the sign's best habit: slow, quiet attention that finds what a fast look misses.

A second pairing is the [Six of Pentacles](/tarot-card-meanings/six-of-pentacles), which is about fair exchange and practical help. It matches the Virgo habit of giving help that can be measured. If you want a card for the day, [draw a free tarot card](/free-tarot). It takes under a minute and needs no account. Tarot is a reflection tool and does not predict events.

## Compatibility for an August 24 birthday

Earth signs read as the easiest match for Virgo, because Taurus and Capricorn move at the same steady pace. Water signs such as Cancer and Scorpio add the emotional warmth that Virgo can underplay. Fire and air signs can work too, though Virgo tends to need patience with their speed.

## Using the date well

Late August is back-to-routine season. Many Virgos feel at home in it: schedules reset, desks clear and new notebooks come out. If your birthday is August 24, that timing may feel natural to you. A simple use of it is a written review of the year so far, with a short list of what you want to keep and one habit you want to drop.

## August 24 zodiac questions

**What zodiac sign is August 24?**

Virgo. The sign runs from about August 23 to September 22, and August 24 is always inside it.

**Is August 24 Leo or Virgo?**

Virgo. Leo ends around August 22, so only August 22 and 23 can fall on either side in a given year.

**What is the element of an August 24 birthday?**

Earth. Virgo is an earth sign, along with Taurus and Capricorn.

**What planet rules an August 24 birthday?**

Mercury. It rules Virgo and also rules the first decan, which covers this date.

**What tarot card matches an August 24 birthday?**

The Hermit is the usual pairing for Virgo. The Six of Pentacles also fits the sign's practical side.
