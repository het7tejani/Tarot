Cancer zodiac dates are usually listed as June 21 to July 22 in Western tropical astrology. Dates well inside that range, such as June 28 and July 4, fall within Cancer. A birthday near June 20 or 21, or July 22 or 23, needs the birth year and exact time checked before the Sun sign is confirmed.

This guide covers the date range, the edge dates and what defines the sign. It uses the Western tropical zodiac, the system most horoscope sites follow.

## Cancer date range

Cancer is the fourth sign of the zodiac. The Sun enters it around June 20 or 21, at the summer solstice in the Northern Hemisphere, and leaves around July 22 or 23. In most years the range is June 21 to July 22.

Quick check: July 10 is well inside the usual Cancer range. June 21 and July 23 sit near sign changes, so do not use the calendar date alone to settle those birthdays.

## Why the dates shift

The Sun takes about 365.24 days to move around the zodiac. Leap years reset the count, so the exact moment of the solstice slides between June 20 and June 21. Cancer starts on that moment, and the exit date moves with it between July 22 and July 23.

For a birthday on June 20, June 21, July 22 or July 23, use the full birth date, recorded birth time and birthplace in an accurate chart calculation. The location helps convert local birth time correctly. Our [birth chart guide](/birth-chart) explains those inputs; it does not calculate a chart or confirm an edge birthday.

## Cancer on the cusp

A birthday near the edge gets the label "Gemini-Cancer cusp" or "Cancer-Leo cusp". Astrology does not treat the cusp as a separate sign. Your Sun occupies one sign at a given moment. Recognizing traits associated with a neighboring sign does not establish a second Sun sign.

Read the [Gemini dates](/blog/gemini-zodiac-dates) and the [Leo dates](/blog/leo-zodiac-dates) next to your birthday if you are close to an edge.

## What defines Cancer

Cancer is a water sign and a cardinal sign, which means it starts a season. The Moon rules it, the fastest-moving body in the sky and the one tied to mood and memory. Astrology describes Cancer as protective, intuitive and tied to home, with a hard shell that protects a soft center.

The usual critique is moodiness. A Cancer who holds a grudge for six months is the classic case. The strength is the same trait pointed outward: Cancer remembers your birthday, your coffee order and the name of your childhood dog.

In the common Golden Dawn-derived tarot correspondence, Cancer is linked to [The Chariot](/tarot-card-meanings/the-chariot), a card associated with direction and self-control. The Moon is Cancer's astrological ruler; that does not make the Moon tarot card its zodiac correspondence. Open the full [Cancer sign guide](/zodiac/cancer) for love, work and strengths.

## Cancer compatibility by element

Water signs share Cancer's emotional range: Scorpio and Pisces read as the closest fits. Earth signs such as Taurus and Virgo give Cancer the stability it likes. Fire and air signs can work, though they move faster than Cancer prefers.

## Cancer questions

**What are the Cancer dates?**

Cancer is usually listed as June 21 to July 22 in the tropical zodiac. The exact Sun sign on a boundary date depends on the year, time and local time zone.

**Is June 21 Cancer or Gemini?**

June 21 is near the Gemini-Cancer change. Check the Sun's position for your birth year and time rather than assuming that the same calendar date works in every time zone.

**Is July 22 Cancer or Leo?**

July 22 can fall on either side of the Cancer-Leo change. Use your birth year, recorded birth time and birthplace to check. If the time is missing and the Sun changed signs that day, keep the uncertainty visible.

**What planet rules Cancer?**

The Moon rules Cancer. Astrology links the Moon with emotion, memory and the home.

**Is the cusp a real sign?**

No. Astrology gives you one Sun sign. The cusp label describes a birthday near the edge.
