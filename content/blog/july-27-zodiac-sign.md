If your birthday is July 27, your zodiac sign is Leo. Leo runs from about July 23 to August 22, so July 27 sits four or five days into the sign. The Sun is in Leo on this date every year, including leap years, so there is no cusp to worry about.

This guide covers what the sign says about a July 27 birthday, where the date sits inside Leo season and which tarot card fits it.

## Why July 27 is always Leo

The Sun moves from Cancer into Leo on July 22 or 23. Only those two dates can shift between signs from year to year. July 27 falls well after both, so it stays Leo in every birth year. If you were born on July 22 or 23, a [birth chart](/birth-chart) settles the sign. For July 27, you can stop checking. The full range is laid out in our [Leo zodiac dates guide](/blog/leo-zodiac-dates).

## What a July 27 birthday looks like in Leo

Leo is a fire sign ruled by the Sun. Astrology describes it as the sign of warmth, visibility and loyalty. People born in the first week of Leo are often described as the most openly Leo of the group, since they sit closest to the sign's start and carry its traits without much softening.

In daily life that can look like a person who walks into a room and changes its temperature. A July 27 Leo tends to remember birthdays, take charge of the plan and give credit loudly when it is deserved. They also want the same back. Praise matters to the sign, and being ignored stings more than being disagreed with.

The usual weak point is pride. A Leo can dig in over something small because backing down feels like losing face. Astrology treats this as the price of the sign's confidence: it is the same trait seen from the other side.

## July 27 and the Leo decan

Western astrology splits each sign into three parts of about ten days, called decans. In the modern decan system, the first part belongs to the sign itself. July 27 sits in the first decan of Leo, which runs from about July 23 to August 1. This decan is ruled by the Sun, the sign's own ruler, so the Leo traits run at full strength here.

The other two decans bring in Jupiter and Mars. Jupiter adds generosity in the middle of the sign, and Mars adds drive near the end. A July 27 birthday gets neither, which is why the sign's reputation for plain, bright confidence fits this date well.

## Tarot card for Leo

The card most often paired with Leo is [Strength](/tarot-card-meanings/strength). A person rests a hand on a lion and the lion stays calm. The card is about steady courage, not force, which is the version of Leo that people trust.

A second pairing is [The Sun](/tarot-card-meanings/the-sun), since the Sun rules the sign. It matches the Leo habit of making things feel lighter just by being in them. If you want a card for the day, [draw a free tarot card](/free-tarot). It takes under a minute and needs no account. Tarot is a reflection tool and does not predict events.

## Compatibility for a July 27 birthday

Fire and air signs are the usual easy matches for Leo. Aries and Sagittarius share the same energy and pace. Gemini and Libra add conversation and lightness. Earth and water signs can work too, though Leo may need to slow down for a Taurus or give a Scorpio more privacy than feels natural.

## Using the date well

Late July is the height of summer in the northern hemisphere, and many Leos feel at home in it: long days, plans outdoors, people around. If your birthday is July 27, that timing may feel natural. A simple use of it is to pick one thing you have been quietly good at and let someone see it, instead of waiting to be asked.

## July 27 zodiac questions

**What zodiac sign is July 27?**

Leo. The sign runs from about July 23 to August 22, and July 27 is always inside it.

**Is July 27 Cancer or Leo?**

Leo. Cancer ends around July 22, so only July 22 and 23 can fall on either side in a given year.

**What is the element of a July 27 birthday?**

Fire. Leo is a fire sign, along with Aries and Sagittarius.

**What planet rules a July 27 birthday?**

The Sun. It rules Leo and also rules the first decan, which covers this date.

**What tarot card matches a July 27 birthday?**

Strength is the usual pairing for Leo. The Sun also fits, since it rules the sign.
