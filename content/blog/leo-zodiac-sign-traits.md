The Leo zodiac sign covers birthdays from July 23 to August 22 in most years. Leo is the fifth sign of the zodiac, a fire sign ruled by the Sun, and its symbol is the Lion. If you want the exact boundary dates and the cusp edge cases, our guide to [Leo zodiac dates](/blog/leo-zodiac-dates) lays them out year by year.

Astrology is a reflection system, not a verdict. Two people born on the same day can live very different lives. What the Leo pattern offers is a vocabulary: a way to notice where warmth, pride and creative drive show up in a person.

![A lion in golden light, the settled presence people associate with the Leo zodiac sign](/blog-images/11838539.webp)

## Leo zodiac sign traits at a glance

Leo is fixed fire. The fire shows up as enthusiasm, appetite for life and a need to make something visible. The fixed quality shows up as staying power: Leos tend to hold their ground, keep their promises and finish what they start.

Common Leo traits people recognize:

- Generosity that is practical, not performative: a Leo friend shows up with help, not just words.
- Pride in their work and their people. Being dismissed stings a Leo more than being disagreed with.
- A pull toward the stage, whether that means leading a team, hosting the dinner or building something with their name on it.
- Loyalty with a long memory. Leos remember who stood by them.

The blind spots sit next to the strengths. Pride can harden into stubbornness. The wish to be appreciated can turn into fishing for applause. And fixed fire burns hot: when a Leo feels humiliated, the reaction is often bigger than the moment.

## Strengths and weak points

Leo's real strength is heart. Among the twelve signs, Leo is the one most associated with courage that protects others rather than courage that wins arguments. Many Leos are at their best when someone they love is under pressure.

The weak points are worth naming plainly. Leos can spend energy managing how things look instead of how they are. They can sulk when appreciation does not arrive on schedule. And because they give loyalty as a default, betrayal hits them harder than most.

## Leo in love and friendship

In love, the Leo zodiac sign tends to be warm, direct and all in. Leos usually say what they feel, expect the same back, and want a partner they can be proud of in public. They do well with people who give honest admiration without flattery, and who have a life of their own.

Air signs often feed Leo's fire: Gemini brings play, Libra brings ease, Aquarius brings a worthy sparring partner. Our [Aquarius and Leo compatibility](/blog/aquarius-leo-compatibility) guide covers that pairing in detail. Fire with fire, Leo with Aries or Sagittarius, makes for momentum but needs shared steering.

Friendship with a Leo is usually simple: celebrate their wins, tell them the truth kindly, and do not mock what they care about.

## FAQ

**What dates are the Leo zodiac sign?**
Leo covers July 23 to August 22 in most years. The exact moment the Sun enters and leaves Leo shifts by a few hours each year, so birthdays on July 23 or August 22 are worth checking against the birth year and time.

**What is the Leo zodiac sign known for?**
Leo is known for warmth, generosity, loyalty and a visible creative drive. The sign is ruled by the Sun, and its reputation for confidence comes from that solar symbolism.

**Who is Leo most compatible with?**
Leo often clicks with fellow fire signs Aries and Sagittarius, and with air signs Gemini, Libra and Aquarius. Compatibility guides describe patterns, not rules: any two people can build a good relationship with honesty and effort.

**What is Leo's ruling planet?**
Leo is ruled by the Sun. In astrology that ties the sign to vitality, identity and the wish to shine in a way that matters to the people around them.
