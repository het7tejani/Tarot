Libra season runs from September 23 to October 22 in most years. If your birthday falls between those dates, your zodiac sign is Libra, the seventh sign of the zodiac, an air sign ruled by Venus, symbolized by the scales.

The boundaries wobble by a day from year to year, because the Sun changes signs at a specific moment rather than at midnight on a fixed date. In some years Libra season begins late on September 22; in others it ends early on October 23. The cusp section below covers those edge cases.

![A statue of Lady Justice holding the scales](/blog-images/30483132.webp)

## Libra dates at a glance

**Libra season: September 23 to October 22.**

**Cusp days to double-check: September 22 to 24 and October 22 to 23.**

For any birthday in the middle of the season, the dates above are all you need. For a birthday on the edges, the exact year and time of birth decide the sign. Our [zodiac sign dates](/zodiac-sign-dates) page lists the boundaries for every sign and is the right starting point for a cusp birthday.

## The only sign with an object for a symbol

Every other zodiac sign is a person, an animal or a hybrid of the two. Libra is the scales: the only inanimate symbol in the twelve. That detail turns out to be a decent summary of the sign. Libra people tend to be the weighing mechanism in any room, the ones who hear both sides before anyone else has finished presenting the first one.

As an air sign, Libra lives in the world of ideas and conversation. As a Venus-ruled sign, it wants those ideas to be beautiful and the conversation to be pleasant. Put the two together and you get the sign most associated with taste: Libras notice when the lighting is wrong, the font is wrong or the seating plan will cause trouble, usually before anyone else notices anything at all.

## Libra strengths and blind spots

The strengths are social intelligence, fairness and charm that does not feel performed. A Libra friend remembers that two of their friends dislike each other and quietly arranges the evening so it never becomes a problem. Diplomacy of that quality is a genuine skill, and Libra has it in surplus.

The blind spots live in the same place. All that weighing takes time, so Libra indecision is famous: two dinner options can survive an entire car journey. Conflict avoidance is the deeper one. A sign built for harmony can swallow its own preferences for months to keep the peace, then be genuinely confused about why it feels resentful. ## Born on the Libra cusp

Birthdays from September 22 to 24 sit near the Virgo boundary, and birthdays from October 22 to 23 sit near the Scorpio boundary. On those days, the year and hour of birth decide the sign. A person born on October 23, 1990 can be a Libra while someone born the same date in another year is a Scorpio. A birth chart settles it properly; the [zodiac sign dates](/zodiac-sign-dates) table is the quick check.

## Libra dates and love: who the scales balance with

Libra is partnership-oriented by design; the scales are literally a two-pan instrument. In relationships, Libras are attentive, romantic in the details, and happiest when the relationship itself feels like a well-run household.

The pairings that tend to flow easiest are with fellow air signs and with fire signs that appreciate an audience. ## Questions about Libra dates

**What dates are Libra?**
September 23 to October 22 in most years. The exact start and end shift by about a day because the Sun changes signs at a precise moment.

**Is October 22 a Libra or a Scorpio?**
Usually Libra, but it depends on the year and time of birth. October 22 and 23 are cusp days, and a birth chart is the reliable way to settle them.

**What element is Libra?**
Air. Libra shares the air element with Gemini and Aquarius, which shows up as conversation, ideas and social awareness.

**Who is Libra most compatible with?**
Gemini and Aquarius speak its language, Leo and Sagittarius bring the warmth it enjoys, and Scorpio offers the depth that helps it grow. Each pairing has its own page in our compatibility section.
