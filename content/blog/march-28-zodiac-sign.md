If you were born on March 28, your zodiac sign is Aries. There is no cusp ambiguity to untangle here: Aries season runs from March 21 to April 19, and March 28 sits comfortably inside it. You are a full Aries, first sign of the zodiac, fire element, ruled by Mars.

That "first sign" label is not decorative. Aries opens the astrological year, and people born under it often carry that opener's energy into ordinary life: the first to volunteer, the first to try the new place, the first to say the thing everyone else was circling.

## March 28 zodiac sign dates at a glance

**Aries season: March 21 to April 19.**

**March 28 falls firmly inside Aries, about a week in.**

The exact start of Aries season is the March equinox, which lands on March 20 or 21 depending on the year. By March 28 the Sun is around seven degrees into Aries, so no birth chart is needed to settle the question. ## The March 28 Aries personality

Aries is a fire sign ruled by Mars, the planet named for the Roman god of war. That combination produces people who move toward things instead of around them. A March 28 Aries tends to decide quickly, act quickly, and learn from the consequences rather than from a manual.

The strengths are easy to spot. Aries people are brave in the ordinary ways that matter: they ask for the raise, defend the friend, book the ticket. They are also honest to a fault, and their enthusiasm is genuinely contagious in the first week of any project.

The blind spots follow the same pattern. Patience is the classic Aries homework. A March 28 Aries can mistake speed for progress, lose interest once the exciting part is done, and win an argument at the exact moment winning stops mattering. The ram's horns are a fitting symbol: useful for breaking through, awkward in a china shop.

## Fire sign energy in daily life

Fire signs run warm: quick to ignite, quick to burn through fuel. For a March 28 Aries, that means energy arrives in bursts. The practical skill is not becoming calmer; it is aiming the bursts. Aries does its best work in sprints with a clear finish line, and its worst work in open-ended meetings about future meetings.

![A bonfire burning at dusk among trees](/blog-images/9175262.webp)

Movement helps this sign think. Many Aries people report that their best decisions arrive on a walk, in a gym, or halfway through doing something physical, not at a desk staring at a pros-and-cons list.

## March 28 zodiac sign in love and compatibility

In relationships, Aries loves the way it does everything else: directly. A March 28 Aries will usually tell you where you stand before you think to ask. They want a partner who has their own momentum, because nothing tires an Aries faster than feeling like the only engine in the room.

The pairings that tend to spark easiest are with fellow fire signs and with air signs that feed the flame. ## Questions about the March 28 zodiac sign

**What is the March 28 zodiac sign?**
Aries. Anyone born from March 21 to April 19 is an Aries, and March 28 falls well inside that window.

**Is March 28 a cusp birthday?**
No. The Pisces-Aries cusp sits around March 19 to 21. By March 28 the Sun is firmly in Aries, so there is no cusp influence to account for.

**What element is the March 28 sign?**
Fire. Aries is a fire sign ruled by Mars, which shows up as fast starts, directness and a low tolerance for waiting.

**Who is a March 28 Aries compatible with?**
Leo and Sagittarius match its fire, Gemini and Aquarius keep it curious, and Libra challenges it in the ways that help it grow. Every pairing has its own page in our compatibility section.
