The May 14 zodiac sign is Taurus in Western tropical astrology. It falls well inside Taurus season, rather than near the usual change into Gemini. Taurus is a fixed earth sign associated with Venus, and its traditional themes include steadiness, pleasure and a strong attachment to what feels dependable.

That tells you the Sun-sign framework, not someone's whole personality. Two people with a May 14 birthday can approach life very differently. The rest of a birth chart depends on the year, exact time and place of birth. Astrology offers a symbolic language for reflection; it does not establish character or predict a life.

## Is May 14 Taurus or Gemini?

In the common Western tropical system, May 14 is Taurus. The usual date range runs from about April 20 to May 20, with exact boundary times changing by year. May 14 is far enough from either boundary that the ordinary Sun-sign answer is straightforward.

Other astrological systems, including sidereal approaches, use different reference points. If a chart gives you a different result, check which system it uses before assuming the calculation is wrong. Our [Taurus dates guide](/blog/taurus-zodiac-dates) explains the season and why boundary birthdays need more care.

Knowing the date alone does not give you a rising sign. A chart needs the birth time and location for that calculation. Do not fill those gaps with a guess and then treat the result as personal information.

## Taurus themes for a May 14 birthday

Earth symbolism puts attention on what can be maintained in daily life. A Taurus reflection often starts with concrete questions: what feels secure, what is worth the effort, and what deserves a slower pace? Those questions can be useful even when the familiar sign description does not sound like you.

The fixed modality is traditionally linked with persistence. In everyday terms, that can look like staying with a skill long enough to become good at it. The same theme can also raise a question about flexibility. Are you protecting something valuable, or repeating a routine because changing it feels uncomfortable?

Venus adds the language of beauty and enjoyment. That need not mean expensive tastes. A well-used chair, a familiar song or a meal cooked carefully can carry the same idea: life is experienced through the senses as well as through plans.

## May 14 and the third decan of Taurus

Astrologers divide each sign's thirty degrees into three ten-degree sections called decans. May 14 usually falls in the third decan of Taurus. In the traditional Chaldean decan system, that section is associated with Saturn. Other decan methods assign sub-rulers differently, so the system should be named rather than presented as universal.

A Saturn-themed interpretation adds questions about patience, limits and long-term effort. It does not mean someone born on this day is destined for hardship. You can use the symbolism to review a commitment: does the effort still serve a purpose, and what would make it sustainable?

## The Seven of Pentacles tarot connection

In Golden Dawn-style tarot correspondences, the third decan of Taurus is linked with the Seven of Pentacles. The familiar card shows a person pausing beside a growing plant. Its useful theme is assessment: something has received effort, and now it needs a thoughtful review.

This is a symbolic birthday association, not a personal card drawn for you. It can still offer a good writing prompt. What have you invested in recently? What evidence shows that it is working? What needs to change before you give it more time?

The [Six of Pentacles](/blog/six-of-pentacles-tarot-card-meaning) asks about fair exchange, while the [Three of Pentacles](/blog/three-of-pentacles-tarot-card-meaning) asks about shared work. Read together, the Pentacles suit gives you different questions about practical life without pretending to decide it for you.

## Love and compatibility

Taurus descriptions often value consistency in relationships. The useful part is the question behind that description: what makes you feel able to trust someone? Some people want reliable plans; others need direct conversation. A birthday cannot answer that on your behalf.

Sun-sign compatibility is a broad tradition, not a verdict on a couple. Shared values and respect matter more than whether a list calls two signs a match. Our [Taurus and Scorpio compatibility guide](/blog/taurus-and-scorpio-compatibility) treats the opposite-sign pairing as a reflection on difference rather than a promise about chemistry.

If you are choosing whether to continue a relationship, look at what happens between you. A chart should not excuse pressure or disrespect.

## A practical birthday reflection

Take one ongoing commitment and review it without judging yourself for its pace. Write down the time it takes and the benefit you actually receive. If you want to keep it, choose one adjustment that makes it easier to maintain. If it has run its course, persistence does not require continuing forever.

## Common questions

**What zodiac sign is May 14?**
Taurus in Western tropical astrology, a fixed earth sign associated with Venus.

**What is the tarot card for May 14?**
The Seven of Pentacles is the third-decan Taurus association in Golden Dawn-style correspondences. It is a symbolic link, not a prediction.

**Can I know my Moon sign from May 14 alone?**
No. You need the birth year and sometimes the time to resolve the Moon's position. A full chart also uses the birthplace.
