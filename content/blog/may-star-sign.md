The May star sign question has two answers, because May holds the handover between two signs. If you were born from May 1 to May 20, your star sign is Taurus. If you were born from May 21 to May 31, your star sign is Gemini. One date, May 20 or 21 depending on the year, is the turning point.

That split matters more than people expect. Taurus and Gemini sit next to each other in the zodiac, but they are built differently: one is fixed earth, the other mutable air. A May 10 birthday and a May 25 birthday belong to the same month and almost opposite temperaments.

## May star sign dates at a glance

**May 1 to May 20: Taurus, an earth sign.**

**May 21 to May 31: Gemini, an air sign.**

The exact switch happens at the moment the Sun moves from Taurus into Gemini, which lands on May 20 or 21 most years. If your birthday falls on one of those two days, check the cusp section below.

## Taurus: the May star sign until May 20

Taurus is the second sign of the zodiac, ruled by Venus. People born under it tend to be steady, sensual and stubborn in roughly that order. A Taurus friend is the one who remembers which restaurant you liked two years ago and still orders the same dish.

The strengths are real: patience, loyalty, and a work ethic that outlasts almost everyone else's enthusiasm. The blind spots are real too. Taurus resists change even when change is overdue, and holds grudges the way it holds savings accounts: quietly, and for a long time.

You can read the full picture on our [Taurus zodiac page](/zodiac/taurus), including strengths, blind spots and how the sign lives well.

## Gemini: the May star sign from May 21

Gemini is the third sign, ruled by Mercury, and symbolized by the twins. The old joke says a Gemini is two people; the kinder truth is that a Gemini is one person with two speeds. Curious, talkative and quick to adapt, Gemini collects ideas the way Taurus collects objects.

At their best, Gemini people are the best conversationalists in the zodiac and the first to volunteer when a plan changes. At their worst, they start ten things and finish four. Boredom is the enemy; variety is the cure. Our [Gemini zodiac page](/zodiac/gemini) goes deeper into what the sign needs.

## Born on the cusp: May 19 to 22

If your birthday sits on May 19, 20, 21 or 22, your sign depends on the exact year and time of birth. The Sun changes signs at a specific moment, and that moment shifts by several hours from year to year. Someone born on May 21, 1995 can be a Taurus while someone born on May 21, 2000 is a Gemini.

The only honest way to settle a cusp birthday is a birth chart calculated from the date, time and place. Our [zodiac sign dates](/zodiac-sign-dates) page lists the boundaries for every month, and it is the right starting point for any cusp birthday in the calendar.

## May star sign in love and friendship

Taurus and Gemini approach relationships from different ends. Taurus wants roots: consistency, physical affection, a shared routine. Gemini wants movement: conversation, novelty, a partner who surprises them. Neither is wrong; they just need different proof of love.

## Questions about the May star sign

**What is the May star sign?**
May has two star signs. Birthdays from May 1 to May 20 are Taurus; birthdays from May 21 to May 31 are Gemini.

**Is May 21 Taurus or Gemini?**
It depends on the year and the time of birth. The Sun moves into Gemini on May 20 or 21, and the exact hour shifts annually. A birth chart settles it.

**What is the May star sign's element?**
Taurus, covering most of May, is an earth sign. Gemini, covering the last ten days, is an air sign.

**Are May Taurus and May Geminis compatible?**
They can be. Taurus brings steadiness, Gemini brings spark, and the friction between routine and variety is the work of that relationship. The full breakdown is on our [Taurus and Gemini compatibility](/zodiac-compatibility/taurus-and-gemini) page.
