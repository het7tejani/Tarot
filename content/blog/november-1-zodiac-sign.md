If your birthday is November 1, your zodiac sign is Scorpio. The sign runs from about October 23 to November 21, so the first day of November sits in the first half of it. There is no cusp to worry about on this date.

This guide covers what Scorpio says about a November 1 birthday, how the date sits at the end of the first decan and which tarot card fits.

## Why November 1 is Scorpio

The Sun enters Scorpio on October 22 or 23, depending on the year, and leaves it around November 21 or 22. November 1 sits well inside that window, so the sign is the same every year. Anyone asking "is November 1 a Libra or Scorpio" can rule Libra out. The dates that need an exact birth-time check are October 22 and 23, plus November 21 and 22 at the other end. For a wider look at the sign, see our guide to the [Scorpio horoscope and tarot cards](/blog/scorpio-horoscope-and-tarot-cards).

Scorpio is the eighth sign and the second water sign. It falls in the part of the year when daylight shortens in the Northern Hemisphere, and many astrologers tie the sign's private streak to that.

## What a November 1 birthday looks like in Scorpio

Scorpio is a fixed water sign. In traditional astrology it is ruled by Mars, and many modern astrologers also name Pluto. The usual description is focused, loyal and guarded. A Scorpio tends to watch a room before speaking and remembers what was said.

The strength of the sign is staying power. A Scorpio finishes what matters to them and does not drop a friend over a small slight. The weak point is secrecy. A Scorpio may say nothing for weeks and then act, which leaves other people guessing. For the relationship side, read our guide to [Scorpio in love](/blog/scorpio-in-love-tarot-and-horoscope), or the [Scorpio sign guide](/zodiac/scorpio).

## November 1 and the Scorpio decan

Each sign splits into three decans of about ten days. In the modern system the first Scorpio decan runs from about October 23 to November 1 and is ruled by Mars. The second runs from about November 2 to November 11 and borrows from Pisces. The third runs from about November 12 to November 21 and borrows from Cancer.

November 1 is the last day of the first decan. A person born on this date is often read as pure Mars Scorpio, with the Pisces softness one day away. Astrologers draw the decan lines differently, and the exact edge depends on the system. None of them changes your Sun sign. ## Tarot card for a November 1 birthday

[Death](/tarot-card-meanings/death) is the card most often paired with Scorpio. It stands for endings and change, not literal death, and it suits a sign that rebuilds itself more than once.

[The Moon](/tarot-card-meanings/the-moon) also fits, since it deals with what is hidden and not yet clear. To pull a card for your own day, [draw a free tarot card](/free-tarot). It takes under a minute and needs no account. Tarot is a reflection tool and does not predict events.

## Compatibility for a November 1 birthday

Water and earth signs usually read as the easiest match for Scorpio. Cancer and Pisces share the emotional depth. Virgo and Capricorn share the practical streak. Taurus sits directly opposite Scorpio, and the pair tends to be loyal and intense.

## Using the date well

The start of November is a quiet point in the calendar. The holiday rush is over, the days are short and many people turn to planning. A simple use of the date is to pick one habit to keep through winter and one commitment to let go of.

## November 1 zodiac questions

**What zodiac sign is November 1?**

Scorpio. The sign runs from about October 23 to November 21, and November 1 is always inside it.

**Is November 1 Libra or Scorpio?**

Scorpio. Libra ends around October 22 or 23.

**What decan is November 1?**

The first Scorpio decan, which ends on November 1 in the modern system. It is ruled by Mars.

**What element is a November 1 birthday?**

Water. Scorpio is a water sign, along with Cancer and Pisces.

**What tarot card matches a November 1 birthday?**

Death is the usual pairing for Scorpio. The Moon also fits.
