Scorpio in love is intense, private and loyal. That is the short version of the Scorpio love horoscope, and it holds across most of astrology. The sign runs from about October 23 to November 21, and people born in that window tend to want depth or nothing.

This guide covers how Scorpio behaves in relationships, which signs usually match well, and which tarot cards readers connect to the sign. For a forecast on a given day, use the [Scorpio horoscope](/horoscope/scorpio) or the [love horoscope page](/love-horoscope).

## How Scorpio acts in love

Scorpio is a fixed water sign. Fixed means it holds on. Water means it feels everything, and shows very little of it at first.

A Scorpio will watch you for weeks before it says anything personal. The watching is a test of whether you keep what you hear. Once trust is there, Scorpio becomes the most committed partner of the zodiac and expects the same in return.

The weak spot is control. Scorpio hates being surprised, so it asks questions that sound like interrogation. Jealousy shows up when a partner is vague. The fix is simple and boring: say where you are and who you are with, and a Scorpio relaxes.

## Best matches for Scorpio

Traditional astrology pairs Scorpio with the other water signs and with its opposite.

Cancer gives Scorpio the safe home it wants and talks about feelings without being asked. Pisces matches Scorpio's depth and adds softness. Our [Scorpio and Pisces compatibility page](/zodiac-compatibility/scorpio-and-pisces) covers that pair in full. Taurus sits across the wheel and shares the need for loyalty. Capricorn gives steadiness, and the [Scorpio and Capricorn page](/zodiac-compatibility/scorpio-and-capricorn) shows where the two clash.

The harder pairings are Leo and Aquarius, the other fixed signs. Three stubborn people in one argument rarely end well.

## Tarot cards linked to Scorpio

Most tarot systems link Scorpio to the Death card. It shows endings that clear space, which fits a sign that cuts ties completely and rebuilds from scratch. Read the full meaning on our [Death card page](/tarot-card-meanings/death).

Some readers also tie Scorpio to the Tower, because Mars is the traditional ruler of the sign, and to the Moon for its hidden side. In a love reading, a Scorpio querent who draws Death usually has a question about letting go of a pattern, not about a person leaving.

## Scorpio and tarot for love questions

If you are Scorpio and want a love reading, ask about what you are holding onto, not what your partner is thinking. Cards describe your side of a situation well. They do not read another person's mind.

A simple three card spread works: what I am protecting, what I am afraid to say, what would help. Our [love tarot reading](/love-tarot-reading) lets you try a spread online. If you want to go deeper on a specific bond, the [soulmate reading](/soulmate-reading) looks at one relationship in detail.

## What to do with this

Use the Sun sign as a starting point, not a verdict. Your Venus sign shows how you love and your Moon sign shows what you need. A [birth chart](/birth-chart) gives you both in one place. Two Scorpios with different Venus signs can love in completely different ways.

## FAQ

**What are Scorpio dates?**

About October 23 to November 21.

**Who is Scorpio most compatible with?**

Cancer and Pisces are the usual first picks, with Taurus and Capricorn close behind.

**What tarot card is Scorpio?**

Most systems link Scorpio to the Death card.

**Is Scorpio loyal in relationships?**

Scorpio is among the most loyal signs once trust is built. It expects the same from a partner.
