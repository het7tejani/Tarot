The Scorpio zodiac sign covers birthdays from October 23 to November 21 in most years. Scorpio is the eighth sign of the zodiac, a water sign with the Scorpion as its symbol. Modern astrology assigns it Pluto as a ruler, while older traditions give it Mars. If your birthday sits near the boundary, our [October 31 zodiac sign](/blog/october-31-zodiac-sign) and [November 1 zodiac sign](/blog/november-1-zodiac-sign) guides walk through the season, and the cusp questions behave like the ones in our [November 2 zodiac sign](/blog/november-2-zodiac-sign) post.

One thing first: astrology describes patterns, not destinies. Scorpio's reputation for intensity is a vocabulary, and like all vocabulary it fits some people better than others.

## Scorpio zodiac sign traits at a glance

Scorpio is fixed water. The water shows up as emotional depth and strong intuition about other people. The fixed quality shows up as focus: when a Scorpio commits to a person, a project or a question, they stay with it long after others get bored.

Traits people commonly recognize in Scorpio:

- Privacy as a default. A Scorpio usually knows more about you than you know about them, and that is by design.
- Emotional X-ray vision. Many Scorpios register a mood shift in a room before anyone has said a word.
- All-or-nothing loyalty. They test people quietly, and once someone passes, the commitment runs deep.
- Comfort with difficult topics. Scorpios are often the friend people call at 2 AM, because nothing about the dark shocks them.

The shadow side deserves plain language too. Scorpios can hold grudges past their usefulness. Secrecy can curdle into control. And the same intensity that makes them loyal can make them suspicious of good faith.

## Strengths and weak points

Scorpio's real strength is transformation. This is the sign most associated with endings that become beginnings: grief that turns into wisdom, failure that turns into strategy. Many Scorpios rebuild themselves more than once in a lifetime, and they rarely advertise the process.

The weak points: jealousy, scorekeeping, and a habit of testing people instead of asking them directly. A Scorpio who feels betrayed often withdraws first and explains never, which can end relationships that an honest conversation might have saved.

## Scorpio in love and friendship

In love, the Scorpio zodiac sign wants depth or nothing. Casual works for a while, but Scorpio's steady state is fusion: shared secrets, shared plans, and a partner who is not afraid of intensity. They need honesty more than they need comfort, and they usually forgive the truth faster than a kind lie.

Water sign pairings tend to feel natural. As friends, Scorpios are few but fierce. They keep confidences, remember the details, and show up in a crisis with a plan.

## Scorpio season and the Death card

Scorpio season runs from late October to late November, the part of the year when the northern hemisphere turns inward. That seasonal mood is not a coincidence: astrologers built Scorpio's symbolism around decay, compost and renewal.

In tarot, Scorpio is traditionally linked with the Death card, which almost never means literal death in a reading. It means a chapter closing so another can start. Our [Scorpio horoscope and tarot cards](/blog/scorpio-horoscope-and-tarot-cards) guide goes deeper into that connection.

## FAQ

**What dates are the Scorpio zodiac sign?**
Scorpio covers October 23 to November 21 in most years. Boundary birthdays can shift by a day depending on the year, so October 23 and November 21 babies should check their exact birth time against an ephemeris.

**What is the Scorpio zodiac sign known for?**
Scorpio is known for intensity, privacy, loyalty and emotional perception. It is a fixed water sign, symbolized by the Scorpion.

**Who is Scorpio most compatible with?**
Scorpio often pairs well with fellow water signs Cancer and Pisces, and with earth signs like Taurus and Capricorn who match their commitment. Compatibility describes tendencies, not guarantees.

**What is Scorpio's ruling planet?**
Modern astrology gives Scorpio Pluto, associated with transformation. Traditional astrology assigns Mars, associated with drive and confrontation. Many astrologers read both.
