A standard tarot deck holds 78 cards, and every tarot card meaning grows from one of two roots. The 22 Major Arcana tell the big story: turning points, lessons and forces larger than one afternoon. The 56 Minor Arcana handle daily life: work, money, feelings, conflict and rest, sorted into four suits.

Learning the deck is less about memorizing 78 definitions and more about learning the grammar underneath them. Once the structure clicks, an unfamiliar card stops being a blank. This guide maps the structure and links out to the deeper card-by-card readings.

## The Major Arcana: the 22 card journey

The Major Arcana run from 0 to 21 and read as one arc, often called the Fool's journey. [The Fool](/blog/the-fool-tarot-card-meaning) sets out with nothing but willingness. Along the way he meets teachers: [The Magician](/blog/the-magician-tarot-card-meaning) shows him tools, [The High Priestess](/blog/the-high-priestess-tarot-card-meaning) shows him silence, [The Empress](/blog/the-empress-tarot-card-meaning) and [The Emperor](/blog/the-emperor-tarot-card-meaning) show him nurture and structure.

The middle of the arc tests him. [The Chariot](/blog/the-chariot-tarot-card-meaning) asks for directed will. [Strength](/blog/strength-tarot-card-upright-meaning) asks for the gentler kind of power. [The Hanged Man](/blog/the-hanged-man-tarot-card-meaning) asks him to trade motion for perspective. The late arc holds the cards people fear most, and they are kinder than their reputations: Death is an ending that clears ground, The Tower is the structure that was never sound, and [The Star](/blog/the-star-tarot-card-meaning) follows it with honest hope.

The journey closes with [The Sun](/blog/the-sun-tarot-card-meaning), Judgement and The World: clarity, reckoning and completion. When a Major card appears in a reading, tradition says to weigh it heavily. It usually marks the theme, while the Minor cards around it supply the details.

## The Minor Arcana: four suits of daily life

Each suit runs Ace through Ten, then Page, Knight, Queen and King.

Wands are fire: drive, work, appetite and the will to start. The [Nine of Wands](/blog/nine-of-wands-yes-or-no) shows the suit's later stage, guarded persistence near the finish.

Cups are water: feelings, bonds and intuition. The suit covers everything from the first spark of affection to the grief of the Five and the reunion of the Six.

Swords are air: thought, truth and conflict. The [Knight of Swords](/blog/knight-of-swords-tarot-card-meaning) is the suit at full speed, decisive and sometimes too fast for its own good.

Pentacles are earth: money, body, craft and the material world. The suit rewards patience, from the skill-building [Three of Pentacles](/blog/three-of-pentacles-tarot-card-meaning) through the self-sufficient [Nine of Pentacles](/blog/nine-of-pentacles-tarot-card-meaning) and the stewardship of the [Queen of Pentacles](/blog/queen-of-pentacles-tarot-card-meaning).

## Upright and reversed meanings

A card drawn upside down is called reversed, and readers handle reversals in two common ways. Some read them as the card's energy blocked, delayed or turned inward. Others read them as the card's shadow: the same lesson arriving through difficulty.

Neither school is mandatory, but consistency matters. If you read reversals, a reversed Sun is still about joy, simply dimmed or delayed, as we cover in [The Sun reversed](/blog/the-sun-reversed-dimmed-light). The card does not become its opposite. It becomes a question about what is blocking the upright meaning.

Court cards add one more wrinkle. Pages, Knights, Queens and Kings can read as people in your life, as roles you are playing, or as stages of the suit's lesson. Context decides, and the surrounding cards give the context.

## How to actually learn the 78 cards

Three methods work better than flashcards. First, learn the suits as four sentences: Wands act, Cups feel, Swords think, Pentacles build. Then each number is just a stage in that sentence, from the Ace's seed to the Ten's full load.

Second, pull one card a day and write two lines about where you saw it by evening. A card met in life is remembered; a card met in an app is trivia. The [free tarot draw](/free-tarot) gives you one card with a reflection question in under a minute.

Third, read the Majors as a story in order once. The journey structure does the memorizing for you, because each card's meaning is its place in the arc.

## Common questions about tarot card meanings

**How many tarot cards are there?**
A standard deck has 78: 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana in four suits of 14.

**Do reversed cards mean something bad?**
No. Reversals point to blockage, delay, inner work or the shadow of the upright meaning. They are information, not doom.

**Which card should a beginner learn first?**
Start with the Major Arcana in order, then the suits one at a time. The structure teaches faster than alphabetical lists.

Tarot is a mirror with a very old frame. Learn the 78 shapes it makes, and the reflection starts answering questions you did not know how to ask.
