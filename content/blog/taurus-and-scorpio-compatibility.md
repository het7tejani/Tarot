Taurus and Scorpio sit directly opposite each other on the zodiac wheel, six signs apart. Astrologers call this an opposition. It is one of the most talked-about pairings because the two signs share a lot and still push in different directions.

This guide covers how Taurus and Scorpio get along in love, where they clash and which tarot cards fit each side. Compatibility in astrology is a reflection tool, not a verdict on any real couple.

## Taurus and Scorpio at a glance

Taurus is a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus. It values comfort, routine, loyalty and things that last. Scorpio is a fixed water sign ruled by Mars in traditional astrology, with Pluto named by many modern astrologers. It values depth, privacy and emotional honesty.

Both are fixed signs, which means both dig in. Neither changes direction quickly. That is the main source of the pair's strength and also of its stalemates. For the full profile of each sign, see our guides to [Taurus](/zodiac/taurus) and [Scorpio](/zodiac/scorpio).

## Why the pair attracts

Opposite signs often feel pulled to each other because each one has what the other lacks. Taurus brings calm and steady habits. Scorpio brings intensity and a willingness to talk about hard things. Many people in this pairing describe a strong first spark followed by a slow, serious bond.

Loyalty is the shared value. Taurus stays because it made a promise. Scorpio stays because it chose carefully. When both feel safe, the relationship tends to be faithful and long.

## Where Taurus and Scorpio clash

The friction usually comes from three sources. Taurus wants a predictable life, and Scorpio wants emotional truth even when it disrupts the routine. Taurus can read Scorpio's silence as coldness. Scorpio can read Taurus's calm as a refusal to engage.

Jealousy and possessiveness are the other common topic. Both signs hold tightly to what they love. Without clear talk about trust, the grip can feel like control. Our guide to [Scorpio in love](/blog/scorpio-in-love-tarot-and-horoscope) covers how the sign handles trust and loyalty in more detail.

## Tarot cards for Taurus and Scorpio

[The Hierophant](/tarot-card-meanings/the-hierophant) is the card often linked with Taurus. It stands for tradition, shared values and commitments kept over time. [The Emperor](/tarot-card-meanings/the-emperor) also fits, since it deals with structure and steady authority.

[Death](/tarot-card-meanings/death) is the card most paired with Scorpio. It stands for endings and change, not literal death. [The Moon](/tarot-card-meanings/the-moon) fits the sign's hidden side. Read together, the cards describe a pair where one side builds and the other transforms. You can [draw a free tarot card](/free-tarot) to reflect on your own question about the match.

## Taurus and Scorpio in friendship and work

As friends, the two tend to be few in number and long in duration. Neither collects acquaintances. They trust slowly, so early meetings can feel stiff, and the bond deepens after a few years.

At work, Taurus brings patience and follow-through, and Scorpio brings focus and a sharp eye for what is not being said. The risk is a standoff: two fixed signs who both think their plan is right. A clear owner for each decision helps.

## Tips for the pair

Say the quiet part out loud. Taurus can name a worry before it becomes a habit of distance. Scorpio can share a feeling before it turns into a test. Agree on small routines, such as one fixed evening a week, so Taurus gets stability and Scorpio gets time with a person who is fully present.

When an argument stalls, take a break before the next round. Fixed signs rarely lose a fight by walking away for an hour, and they often settle it faster after.

## Taurus and Scorpio compatibility questions

**Are Taurus and Scorpio compatible?**

They can be. The signs share loyalty and staying power, and they differ in pace and in how they handle emotion. Compatibility depends on the two people, not only on the Sun signs.

**Why are Taurus and Scorpio opposites?**

They sit six signs apart on the zodiac wheel, which astrologers call an opposition. Opposite signs share a quality but sit at opposite poles. Here both signs are fixed.

**What is the biggest problem for Taurus and Scorpio?**

Stubbornness. Both are fixed signs, so each can hold a position long after it stops helping. Jealousy and silence are the other common friction points.

**Which tarot cards fit Taurus and Scorpio?**

The Hierophant and The Emperor are often linked with Taurus. Death and The Moon are often linked with Scorpio.
