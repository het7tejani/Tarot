Taurus season runs from April 20 to May 20, and the Taurus personality is the zodiac's steadiest. Ruled by Venus and fixed in earth, this is the sign that plants something and is still watering it a decade later. Ask a room of astrologers to describe Taurus and the same words come back: patient, loyal, sensual, stubborn.

Those four words are true, but they are also the shallow version. Here are seven Taurus traits with the texture left in, including the two the sign would rather skip.

## 1. Steady before anything else

The defining Taurus trait is pace. Taurus does not rush, does not like being rushed, and quietly distrusts people who do. This reads as slowness until you watch a Taurus finish a project everyone else abandoned in week two. Fixed earth means the energy goes deep rather than wide: one garden, tended properly, beats five half-dug plots.

## 2. Sensory by design

Venus gives Taurus the most physical temperament in the zodiac. Good food, real fabric, a room that smells right: these are not luxuries to a Taurus, they are maintenance. This is the sign most likely to have a strong opinion about thread count and the least likely to apologize for it.

## 3. Loyal to a fault, and this time the fault is real

Taurus loyalty is famous, and mostly deserved. The fault hides in the same place: Taurus stays. It stays in jobs that stopped growing, in friendships that stopped giving, in arguments it already lost, because leaving feels like waste. The loyalty is genuine; so is the cost.

## 4. Stubborn, which is patience with the brakes cut

Every Taurus trait list mentions stubbornness, and every Taurus calls it something else: consistency, standards, knowing their own mind. The honest version is that Taurus decides slowly and then treats the decision as load-bearing. Arguing a Taurus out of a position takes evidence, time and snacks, in that order.

## 5. Good with money, boring about it, on purpose

Taurus has the zodiac's most practical relationship with money. The pattern is consistent: earn steadily, spend on quality once instead of junk twice, keep a cushion. A Taurus budget is rarely exciting and rarely wrong. Where the sign trips is conflating security with accumulation, holding on to things and arrangements past their usefulness.

## 6. Calm until the exact moment it is not

Taurus temper has a long fuse and a memorable ending. The sign absorbs irritation for months, files it, says nothing. Then one small thing lands on the pile and the bull arrives. People who know a Taurus well learn to ask early, because the sign will not volunteer the complaint until it has become a case file.

## 7. Romantic in the old sense

In love, Taurus shows rather than tells. The sign's affection is practical and physical: cooked meals, fixed shelves, a hand on your back in a crowd. Grand speeches bore it. ## Taurus and tarot

In the Major Arcana, Taurus is traditionally linked to [The Hierophant](/tarot-card-meanings/the-hierophant), the card of tradition, teachers and inherited wisdom, which fits the sign's respect for what has been tested by time. Its ruling planet Venus ties it to [The Empress](/tarot-card-meanings/the-empress), the card of abundance and the senses. A Taurus asking one card for guidance usually needs the same answer: what are you holding that has already finished?

## Questions about the Taurus personality

**What are the main Taurus personality traits?**
Steady, patient, loyal, sensory and stubborn. Taurus is an earth sign ruled by Venus, which makes it both the most grounded and the most comfort-loving sign in the zodiac.

**What are Taurus dates?**
Taurus season runs from April 20 to May 20. Birthdays on the boundary days should check a birth chart, since the exact changeover hour shifts each year; our [zodiac sign dates](/zodiac-sign-dates) page has every month's boundaries.

**What is Taurus's biggest weakness?**
Resistance to change. The same staying power that makes Taurus reliable keeps it in situations past their end date. Growth for Taurus usually means leaving something on purpose for once.
