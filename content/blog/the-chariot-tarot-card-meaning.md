The Chariot tarot card meaning is victory through direction: two forces pulling against each other, and a driver who makes them pull together. The Chariot is card 7 of the Major Arcana, and it arrives after [the Lovers](/tarot-card-meanings/the-lovers), the card of choosing. The Chariot is what you do after you have chosen: you commit, you steer, and you move.

In the Rider-Waite deck an armored figure stands in a stone chariot under a canopy of stars. Two sphinxes, one black and one white, crouch in front of the chariot, and there are no reins in the driver's hands. A square sits on his chest, crescent moons rest on his shoulders, and a city wall stands behind him. He is leaving safety on purpose.

## What the Chariot card shows

The two sphinxes are the heart of the card. Black and white, pulling in different directions, they stand for every pair of opposites a person carries: ambition and comfort, head and heart, the wish to stay and the wish to go. Most cards ask you to resolve the tension. The Chariot asks you to harness it.

The missing reins matter. The driver does not control the sphinxes by force. He controls them by will, by being so clear about the destination that both forces fall in behind it. The armor and the walled city complete the picture: protection exists, but the card shows the moment you roll out past it.

The starry canopy above the chariot ties the journey to guidance. Willpower steers, but the driver is still following something.

## The Chariot upright meaning and keywords

Upright Chariot keywords include direction, determination and self-control. Readers often connect the card with purposeful action, but it cannot promise success. Check whether your goal is clear and whether the resources and agreements needed to pursue it are actually in place.

The card often appears during conflicts that need steady handling: a competitive process, a hard negotiation, a move, a habit being broken. The Chariot's advice is to stop relitigating the decision and put the energy into the driving.

It can also mean literal travel, a vehicle, or a period of fast movement after standing still.

## The Chariot in love and work

In love, the Chariot upright suggests someone pursuing what they want with unusual clarity. If you are asking about a relationship, the card asks a harder question: are you steering this, or being carried? The Chariot rewards a person who chooses the relationship actively, and it warns against mistaking speed for closeness.

In work, this is the promotion card, the launch card, the "push through the final stretch" card. Competition is part of the picture and the Chariot favors you in it, as long as you do not swerve to fight every rival. Pick the line and hold it.

## The Chariot reversed

Reversed, the Chariot points to a vehicle out of control: aggression without aim, effort spent in five directions, or the sphinxes fighting while the driver watches. It can also mean the opposite problem, total stall, where the goal was chosen once but nobody is steering anymore.

The remedy is to stop and re-aim before pressing the accelerator again. Direction first, speed second. The card also asks you to check whether you are trying to control what cannot be controlled, other people's choices chief among them.

For a faster court-card expression of the same directed energy, compare it with the [Knight of Swords](/blog/knight-of-swords-tarot-card-meaning). The Chariot sustains control; the Knight acts on a decision quickly. ## Common questions about the Chariot

**Is the Chariot a yes or no card?**
Most readers treat the Chariot as a yes, especially for questions about competition, moves and new ventures, with the condition that you commit fully.

**What does the Chariot mean for the near future?**
Forward motion after a standstill, and a stretch where discipline pays. Expect the pace to pick up and plan to steer rather than react.

**What zodiac sign belongs to the Chariot?**
The Chariot is linked to Cancer, the sign of the protective shell and the tenacious grip, a fitting patron for a card about defending your direction.
