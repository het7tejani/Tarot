The High Priestess tarot card meaning is the knowledge you already have but have not said out loud yet. She is card 2 of the Major Arcana, sitting between [the Magician](/tarot-card-meanings/the-magician), who acts, and the Empress, who creates. The High Priestess does neither. She sits still, and in the tarot that stillness is a form of power.

In the Rider-Waite deck a woman in blue robes sits on a stone throne between two pillars, one black marked B, one white marked J. Behind her hangs a veil decorated with pomegranates. A crescent moon rests at her feet, a cross sits on her chest, and a scroll marked TORA lies half-hidden in her lap. Everything in the card is quiet, and everything means something.

![A woman holding the moon, the quiet knowing of the High Priestess](/blog-images/10524858.webp)

## What the High Priestess card shows

The two pillars come from the temple of Solomon: Boaz and Jachin, darkness and light, the pair of opposites that guard every mystery. The High Priestess sits between them rather than choosing a side. Her message is that the truth she guards includes both.

The veil of pomegranates separates the visible world from what sits behind it. In the old myth, the pomegranate belonged to Persephone, who crossed between worlds. The fruit says the hidden realm is real and the veil can be crossed, but not by force and not yet.

The scroll in her lap is partly covered. Readers take that literally: some of what you need to know is available now, and some of it stays rolled up until you are ready. The crescent moon at her feet ties her to cycles, tides and the kind of knowing that arrives in feelings before it arrives in words.

## The High Priestess upright

Upright, the High Priestess tarot card meaning is: listen inward. The answer you are looking for is not in another opinion, another search, another pro and con list. It is in the quiet sense you have been carrying while you keep asking everyone else.

The card often appears when something is not being said. A situation has a hidden layer, a person has an unspoken motive, or you have information you have not admitted to yourself. The High Priestess asks you to trust your read of the room before the facts catch up.

She also counsels patience. The Magician acts; the High Priestess waits, and her waiting is active. When she appears, forcing a reveal usually backfires. Let the situation show itself.

## The High Priestess in love and work

In love, the High Priestess upright often describes attraction with a strong unspoken current: things felt but not declared. Her advice is to watch and sense rather than interrogate. If you are asking about a relationship, she can also flag that something is being kept from you, kindly or otherwise, and that your intuition has already noticed.

In work, she favors research, study and quiet preparation over announcements. Keep the plan private while it forms. She is also a good omen for anyone whose work depends on insight: analysts, writers, healers, readers.

## The High Priestess reversed

Reversed, the High Priestess points to intuition blocked or ignored. You may be drowning out your inner voice with noise, other people's opinions, or plain busyness. Or the secrecy in the situation has curdled: hidden motives, withheld information, gossip where there should be plain speech.

The fix is deliberate quiet. Ten honest minutes alone will tell you more than ten more conversations. If the card describes another person, take their silence as information too.

## Common questions about the High Priestess

**Is the High Priestess a yes or no card?**
Most readers read her as "not yet" or "you already know". She rarely gives a flat yes or no because her whole message is that the answer is internal.

**What does the High Priestess mean about someone's feelings?**
Strong, real and mostly unspoken. The person feels more than they show and is watching before they move.

**What zodiac sign and planet belong to the High Priestess?**
She is linked to the Moon, which governs instinct, memory and the parts of the mind that work below the surface.
