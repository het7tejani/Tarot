Hi, I'm Daisy Hayes. I started The Psychic Studio to make tarot and astrology feel clear, calm and useful, not mysterious for the sake of it.

## Start with a free tarot reading

If you are new, the quickest way in is a [free tarot reading online](/free-tarot). Draw one card for a quick answer or three cards for past, present and future. Each card comes with upright and reversed meanings and a question to reflect on.

## Learn what each card means

Every card in the deck has its own page, from The Fool to the Ten of Pentacles. Read the [tarot card meanings](/tarot-card-meanings) for love, career and daily life. Good places to begin are [The Moon](/tarot-card-meanings/the-moon) and [The Tower](/tarot-card-meanings/the-tower).

## Your daily horoscope and love horoscope

Check your [daily horoscope](/horoscope) for a short reflection each morning, or read the [love horoscope](/love-horoscope) for each zodiac sign. If you keep seeing repeating numbers, the [angel numbers guide](/angel-numbers) explains what they may be telling you.

## Personal psychic readings

When you want a reading made for you, my readings are in the [shop](/shop). Each one links to its Etsy listing, where you can check the details and order safely.

Readings are for entertainment and personal reflection. They are not a replacement for medical, legal, financial or mental health advice.
